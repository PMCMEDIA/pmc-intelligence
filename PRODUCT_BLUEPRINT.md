# PMC Intelligence — Product Blueprint

## Core workflow

Research → Discovery → Competitive Intelligence → Strategy → Department Workspaces → Benchmark & Forecast Engine → Investment Builder → KPI / ROI Outlook → Deck Builder

## Competitive Intelligence

Competitive research should occur after Discovery and before final strategy/investment approval.

Research should capture observable, source-backed information such as:
- Positioning and messaging
- Services / products
- Geography
- Offers and conversion paths
- Website and landing-page strategy
- SEO visibility
- Content themes
- Social presence
- Review / reputation signals
- Paid-search presence where observable
- Major differentiators

Every research item should retain source and research date. The system must distinguish verified research from inference and PMC recommendation.

## Benchmark hierarchy

Forecasting should prioritize evidence in this order:

1. Client historical performance — Google Ads, Meta, GA4, MNTN, CRM, call tracking, etc.
2. PMC historical benchmarks — comparable clients/campaigns by industry, channel, geography and objective.
3. Current external benchmarks — when first-party/PMC data is insufficient.
4. Modeled assumptions — clearly labeled and never presented as actual historical performance.

## Forecast Engine

Use channel-appropriate formulas rather than generic percentages.

Example paid search:
- Budget ÷ expected CPC = estimated clicks
- Clicks × expected conversion rate = estimated conversions
- Budget ÷ estimated conversions = projected CPA
- Conversions × close rate = estimated customers
- Customers × average customer value = projected revenue
- Projected revenue ÷ media spend = modeled ROAS

Performance Media should use CPM / impressions / response assumptions where appropriate.
Paid Social should use channel-appropriate CPM/CPC and conversion assumptions.
SEO/AEO must use a separate organic visibility and demand model rather than pretending it behaves like paid media.

## Scenario ranges

Do not imply false precision. Forecasts should show:
- Conservative
- Expected
- Upside

Also show:
- Projected CPA range
- Projected ROAS range where revenue inputs support it
- Confidence level
- Primary benchmark source
- Secondary benchmark source
- Benchmark last-updated date
- Key assumptions

## Investment Builder

The budget recommendation should be evidence-based and consider:
- Client objective
- Geography
- Competitive environment
- Historical performance
- Benchmark performance
- Channel minimums
- Campaign duration
- Production / creative requirements
- Capacity constraints
- Conversion definition
- Expected business value

Users must be able to edit channel allocations and have projected KPIs recalculate live.

Digital Media should support internal channel allocation, including appropriate combinations of:
- Google Search
- Performance Max
- Paid Social
- Performance Media / CTV
- Geofencing
- Other approved channels

## KPI / ROI Outlook

The final planning output should include a Modeled Campaign Outlook with appropriate metrics by channel and blended campaign totals.

Possible outputs:
- Impressions / reach
- Clicks / traffic
- Qualified conversions
- Conversion rate
- CPA / CPL
- Customers / appointments / bookings where modeled
- Revenue or pipeline value where inputs support it
- ROAS / ROI where inputs support it

Label modeled outputs as “Projected Performance” or “Modeled Campaign Outlook,” not guaranteed predictions.

## Deck Builder

Approved research, Discovery, department strategies, investment allocation and forecast assumptions should feed the final PMC deck.

The deck should include a Campaign Outlook section showing assumptions, ranges, confidence and benchmark provenance rather than unsupported single-number promises.

## Product principles

- Separate Verified Research, Client-Provided Information, PMC Recommendation and Modeled Assumption.
- Never present modeled assumptions as observed facts.
- Preserve sources and dates.
- Prefer client historical data over generic benchmarks.
- Show uncertainty and confidence.
- Allow editing at every major approval stage.
- Keep completed Discovery collapsed but editable.
- Department strategies must be approved before final investment/deck generation.
